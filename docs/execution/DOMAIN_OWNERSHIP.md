# Domain Ownership

- **catalog**: Product definitions, categories, barcodes.
- **purchasing**: Supplier orders, receiving, invoices.
- **costing**: Landed cost, MWA, pricing reference cost.
- **pricing**: Published retail prices, schedules, tiers, promotions.
- **inventory**: Stock balance, stock movements, opname.
- **sales**: Transactions, items, checkout flows.
- **cash**: Shifts, cash ledger, cash in/out.
- **identity**: Users, roles, permissions, devices.
- **returns**: Voids, returns, exchanges, refunds.
- **sync**: Idempotency, cursors, change feeds, outboxes.
- **reporting**: Read models for analytics.
